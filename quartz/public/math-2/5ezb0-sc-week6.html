7 Gradient in Cartesian coordinates Gradient operator ∇ (or grad): ∇f = grad f =  ˆı ∂ ∂ x + ˆȷ ∂ ∂ y + ˆ k ∂ ∂ z  f Properties: ▶ operates on scalar field f = f (x, y, z) to yield a vector field ▶ direction of ∇f :

13 Diffusion: Fick’s law ϕ(x, t): concentration of particles Jx(x, t): diffusive flux in x-direction (number of particles moving per unit of area per unit of time) Fick’s law in one dimension: Jx(x, t) = -Ddϕ(x, t) dx with D the diffusivity Fick’s law generalized to three dimensions: J = -D∇ϕ -∇ϕ: local direction towards lower concentration

14 Conservative forces (one dimension) ▶ Conservative force at position x can be obtained from potential energy function as function of x Fx(x) = -dU(x) dx ▶ In regions where U(x) changes rapidly with x → large force (magnitude) ▶ When Fx(x) is in positive x-direction, U(x) decreases with increasing x ▶ Conservative force always acts to push the system towards lower potential energy
