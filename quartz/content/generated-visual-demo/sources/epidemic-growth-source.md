# Early-growth intervention relationship

Before susceptible depletion becomes important, infection pressure grows when the effective reproduction factor exceeds one and shrinks when it is below one. In this compact teaching model, a contact-reduction fraction changes the factor according to:

$$
R_{\mathrm{eff}} = R_0(1-c)
$$

The relationship is dimensionless. Intervention timing determines how long pre-intervention growth continues. Any trajectory reconstructed from this relationship is illustrative and must not be interpreted as a calibrated forecast.
