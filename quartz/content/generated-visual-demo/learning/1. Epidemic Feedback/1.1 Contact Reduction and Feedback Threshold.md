# Contact Reduction and Feedback Threshold

An intervention's strength changes the post-intervention reproduction factor. In the illustrative early-growth relationship $R_{\mathrm{eff}}=R_0(1-c)$, values above one indicate growth pressure and values below one indicate decline pressure. This lesson isolates that threshold relationship before a later analysis adds time-dependent trajectories.

## What to notice

Try moving the contact reduction just far enough to cross $R_{\mathrm{eff}}=1$. Then increase the baseline reproduction factor and predict how much additional reduction is needed before moving the second control.
