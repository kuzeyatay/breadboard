| Day               | Main goal                                       | Sections I rewrite                                                                                                                | What you solve                                                                                               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Day 1** | Build the 3D language of the course             | Adams **10.1, 10.5, 10.6**: 3D coordinates, distances, planes/surfaces, quadrics, cylindrical and spherical coordinates           | Coordinate conversion, sketching domains/surfaces, recognizing quadrics                                      |
| **Day 2**         | Understand curves as parametrized motion        | Adams **12.1, 12.3**: vector functions, velocity, acceleration, speed, parametrization, curve length                              | Parametrize curves, compute velocity/acceleration, arc length                                                |
| **Day 3**         | Start multivariable functions properly          | Adams **13.1, 13.2**: domains, graphs, level curves/surfaces, limits, continuity                                                  | Domain sketches, level curves, multivariable limit/non-limit proofs                                          |
| **Day 4**         | Differentiate functions of several variables    | Adams **13.3, 13.4, 13.5**: partial derivatives, tangent planes, normal lines, higher derivatives, chain rule                     | Partial derivative calculations, tangent planes, chain rule trees                                            |
| **Day 5**         | Finish differential calculus                    | Adams **13.6, 13.7, 13.9**: linear approximation, differentiability, gradient, directional derivative, Taylor polynomial degree 2 | Gradient/directional derivative questions, linearization, Taylor approximation                               |
| **Day 6**         | Master double integrals                         | Adams **15.1, 15.2, 15.4**: double integrals, Cartesian regions, changing order, polar coordinates                                | Set up and evaluate double integrals; convert Cartesian ↔ polar                                              |
| **Day 7**         | Master triple integrals and change of variables | Adams **15.5, 15.6, 15.7 surface area only**                                                                                      | Triple integral bounds, cylindrical/spherical integrals, Jacobian/change of variables, surface area of graph |
| **Day 8**         | Enter vector fields                             | Adams **16.1, 16.2, 16.3, 16.4**: scalar/vector fields, conservative fields, line integrals, work integrals                       | Conservative-field tests, potential functions, line integrals along curves                                   |
| **Day 9**         | Surface integrals and flux                      | Adams **16.5, 16.6**: parametric surfaces, surface integrals, oriented surfaces, flux                                             | Parametrize surfaces, compute normal vectors, surface integrals and flux                                     |
| **Day 10**        | Final synthesis before exams                    | Adams **17.1 + theorem roadmap**: gradient, divergence, curl, plus how line/surface/flux concepts connect                         | Mixed review set: domains, gradients, integrals, conservative fields, flux, div/curl                         |
