## Lecture Attendance and Course Resource Feedback

Source: [[979070-english|Diffraction, Interference, and Course Feedback]]

Locations: Page 18, Page 19

The final part of the source records a live feedback activity about attendance, resources, and instructional support. Students are asked which course resources they use, whether they attend live lectures, whether recordings affect attendance, whether commute time matters, and whether lectures add value compared with self-study. The lecturer also asks open questions about what would make students attend lectures or instruction sessions more often. Mentioned factors include clashing deadlines, scheduled courses such as computer architecture and electronic circuits, room preferences, lecture pace, sleep, exam training, and the number of rooms for Thursday afternoon instruction sessions. This material documents course-design feedback rather than physics content, but it captures concrete factors that influence lecture attendance and instructional participation.

### Key points

- Students were asked which course resources they use.
- The lecturer asked whether recordings affect live lecture attendance.
- Commute time was presented as a possible attendance factor.
- Students were asked whether lectures add value compared with self-study.
- Open feedback asked what would increase lecture attendance.
- Instruction sessions on Thursday afternoons were separately discussed.
- More exam training and more instruction rooms appeared as feedback themes.
