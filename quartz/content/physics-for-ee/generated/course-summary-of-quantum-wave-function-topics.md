The instructor states that the course gave a glimpse of what wave functions mean and how they can be used. It covered solutions to the one-dimensional Schrödinger equation, especially the time-independent version where only the spatial part is considered. The lecture also discussed unusual exponential tails, the finite square well, the quantum oscillator, tunneling, and entanglement. This closing summary situates the entanglement discussion within the larger framework of quantum mechanics: wave functions, boundary-value systems, tunneling phenomena, and measurement-linked correlations.
