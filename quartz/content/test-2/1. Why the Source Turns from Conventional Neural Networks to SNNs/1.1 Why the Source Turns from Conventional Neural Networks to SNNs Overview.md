The key contrast is between continuous, coordinated computation and discrete, event-driven spike-based computation. This contrast matters because the paper ties it directly to engineering consequences. If computation is driven by sparse events rather than dense continuous updates, then fewer operations may be needed. That is the source's route from spike-based computation to energy efficiency. The same contrast also explains why the paper emphasizes temporal dynamics. When information arrives as events over time, timing is not merely an extra detail added after the fact; the timing of signals becomes part of the computation itself. For that reason, the source presents SNNs as especially promising for spatiotemporal processing, where both the content of a signal and the moment it occurs matter.
