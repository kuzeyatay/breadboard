## Teaching Interaction Model with Two Instructors

Source: [[988929-english-3|Communication 1 Course Introduction and Foundations of Digital Communication]]

Locations: Page 4, Page 5

The course uses a two-instructor teaching model in which one instructor typically delivers the main lecture content while the other provides recap, problem solving, and interactive support. This structure is presented as a deliberate investment in student learning. The second instructor often begins sessions by recapping the previous lecture, using data from online quizzes or direct classroom interaction to target key points. Later in the session, old exam questions may be solved, sometimes selected based on student requests through Discord. The instructors stress that the pace is not intended to be rushed and that the recap exists to refresh, clarify, and invite questions before new material is built on top of earlier concepts.

### Key points

- Most lectures are led by one instructor, with the second supporting recap and exercises.
- Recaps at the beginning of class revisit the previous lecture.
- Recaps may use data from online quizzes to identify weaknesses.
- Old exam questions can be solved during sessions.
- Student input through Discord can influence which problems are discussed.
- The course is designed to allow time for questions rather than rushing through content.
