## Graded Lab Structure and Lab Quizzes

Source: [[988929-english-3|Communication 1 Course Introduction and Foundations of Digital Communication]]

Locations: Page 4

The course includes several lab weeks that are integrated into regular contact moments. Labs are offered in person, but students may also complete them at home. The instructors explicitly encourage collaborative work during the labs, while still requiring each student to complete graded quiz components. At the end of each lab, or after it, students complete a graded Canvas quiz based on what they experienced during the lab. These lab quizzes contribute to the final grade, making the labs both instructional and formally assessed. The lab support model includes around 30 teaching assistants, underscoring that labs are intended as guided learning environments rather than isolated homework tasks.

### Key points

- The course includes lab weeks embedded in normal contact sessions.
- Labs can be done in person or at home.
- Students are encouraged to work together during labs.
- There are three graded lab quizzes in total.
- The quizzes are delivered on Canvas and count toward the final course grade.
- About 30 teaching assistants support students during lab sessions.
