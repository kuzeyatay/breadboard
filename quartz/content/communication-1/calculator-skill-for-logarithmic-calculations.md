## Calculator Skill for Logarithmic Calculations

Source: [[988934-english-1|Introduction to Decibels, dBm, and dBW in Engineering]]

Locations: Page 2

A procedural theme in the lecture is that students must be able to perform base-10 logarithm calculations on their calculators without assistance. The instructor warns that in every exam some students struggle because they are using their calculator for the first time on logarithmic questions and do not know how to compute $\log_{10}$. This is presented not as a trivial detail but as an essential exam skill. The lecture specifically recommends practicing with simple calculators, including the Casio FX-82 family or equivalent models, and demonstrates the procedure by evaluating $\log_{10}(2)$ and multiplying the result by 10 to get approximately 3 dB. The durable lesson is that conceptual understanding of dB must be paired with tool fluency: students should verify in advance that they can enter logarithms, interpret outputs, and convert between ratios and dB values efficiently.

### Key points

- Students must know how to compute base-10 logarithms on their calculator.
- Exam problems assume calculator fluency with logarithmic scales.
- The lecture warns that instructors will not explain calculator operation during exams.
- A simple procedure is demonstrated: compute $\log_{10}(2)$, then multiply by 10.
- Basic calculators such as the FX-82 family are described as sufficient.
