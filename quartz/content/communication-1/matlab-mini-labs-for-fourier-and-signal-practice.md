## MATLAB Mini-Labs for Fourier and Signal Practice

Source: [[988929-english-3|Communication 1 Course Introduction and Foundations of Digital Communication]]

Locations: Page 9

The lecture describes optional MATLAB mini-labs created by previous teaching assistants to help students explore signal-processing concepts interactively. These mini-labs are not required for course credit, but are positioned as useful tools for students who want deeper understanding. A specific example is given for Fourier series: an interface allows students to inspect harmonics, change their strength, and reconstruct waveforms such as square or triangular waves. The mini-labs exemplify an important pedagogical method in technical subjects: using simulation to make abstract mathematical ideas visible and manipulable. They also align with the course's broader emphasis on self-testing and active exploration.

### Key points

- Optional MATLAB mini-labs are provided as additional practice tools.
- They were created by dedicated teaching assistants in earlier years.
- Students can download apps and run them in MATLAB on their laptops.
- A Fourier-series mini-lab lets students inspect harmonics and adjust amplitudes.
- The mini-labs support deeper conceptual understanding rather than formal assessment.
