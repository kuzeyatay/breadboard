## Importance of Practicing Course Material with Quizzes and Feedback

Source: [[988934-english-1|Introduction to Decibels, dBm, and dBW in Engineering]]

Locations: Page 3, Page 4

The lecture encourages students to reinforce their understanding of decibels through course quizzes and study resources. A quiz in Canvas is specifically mentioned for practicing dBm and dB before the next session, and the instructors say they will begin Thursday with an entry-level dBm quiz to refresh the material. Beyond individual practice, quizzes are described as feedback tools for the instructors: the more students use them, the better the teaching team can gauge the group's level and tailor instruction to actual needs. This reflects a reusable study procedure: review the reader, correct lecture-note mistakes, complete short quizzes even if the content seems familiar, and use the results to identify unclear points. The topic is durable because it captures a practical method for learning technical notation and avoiding predictable errors in later exam work.
