## Course Topic Letter Map

Source: [[topicmap|Communications 1 (5ETC0) Topic Map]]

Locations: Page 1

The page is not only a communication-system diagram but also a course topic map. Several blocks are annotated with letter references such as B,C, D,E, E, i,H, J,K, G, and F,J,K. These letters appear under or near specific parts of the communication chain, indicating how the course topics correspond to stages of the system. Early transmitter concepts such as sampling are associated with B,C, quantization with D,E, PCM or related coding with E and i,H, and modulation or transmission topics with J,K. The raised-cosine filter and ISI/noise region is associated with F,J,K, tying filtering and channel impairment material to later physical-layer concepts. The map therefore functions as an organizing structure for the full course, connecting lecture units to the communication pipeline.
