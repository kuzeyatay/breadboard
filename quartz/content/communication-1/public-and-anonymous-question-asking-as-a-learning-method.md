## Public and Anonymous Question Asking as a Learning Method

Source: [[988934-english-1|Introduction to Decibels, dBm, and dBW in Engineering]]

Locations: Page 1

The lecture begins by framing student questions as a valuable part of the learning process rather than as interruptions. The instructors explicitly encourage students to ask questions in front of the whole group because many other students may share the same misunderstanding but may not dare to ask. Public questioning is presented as beneficial both for peers and for the instructors, since it reveals where explanations were unclear and helps them improve the course. At the same time, the source recognizes that some students are uncomfortable speaking up, so an anonymous alternative through Discord during the break is proposed. This creates a reusable study method: ask questions openly when possible, and otherwise use an anonymous channel so the question can still be discussed collectively. The teaching principle is that clarifying misunderstandings early, especially in a technical course with mathematical conventions and notation, improves both student comprehension and instructional quality.

### Key points

- Students are encouraged to ask questions in front of the whole class.
- Public questions often help other students with the same confusion.
- Questions reveal what the instructors failed to explain clearly.
- Discord is proposed as an anonymous way to submit lecture questions.
- The break is too short to handle every question individually.
- The goal is to create shared space for clarification during class.
