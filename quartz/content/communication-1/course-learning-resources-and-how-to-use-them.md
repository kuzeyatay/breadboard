## Course Learning Resources and How to Use Them

Source: [[988929-english-3|Communication 1 Course Introduction and Foundations of Digital Communication]]

Locations: Page 3, Page 8, Page 9

The instructors describe a broad set of learning resources and repeatedly warn students not to try to consume everything indiscriminately. Available materials include live lectures, recorded lectures, a reader of about 150 pages, podcasts generated from the course text, optional Canvas quizzes, optional MATLAB mini-labs, old exams, and Discord-based question channels. Students are told that attending the current lecture and making their own notes is sufficient for that topic, and that replaying older versions of the same lecture is not necessary unless they missed something. Optional resources are framed as depth tools rather than mandatory tasks. This topic captures a durable study principle: abundant resources are only helpful when students selectively use the ones that match their learning need.

### Key points

- Recorded lectures are available, but students are told not to re-watch older versions unnecessarily.
- A reader of about 150 pages serves as the main reading resource.
- Podcasts were created from the course material for lightweight review.
- Discord is available for asking and sharing questions.
- Optional quizzes and mini-labs are provided for extra practice.
- Students are explicitly told not to try to do everything.
