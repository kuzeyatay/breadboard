## Assessment Structure: Midterm, Final, and Compensation

Source: [[988929-english-3|Communication 1 Course Introduction and Foundations of Digital Communication]]

Locations: Page 2, Page 3

The course uses multiple assessment moments, including a midterm and a final exam, and the midterm is framed primarily as both a grade component and a diagnostic learning opportunity. The instructors explain that the midterm can contribute 20% of the grade, but poor performance can be compensated by stronger performance on the final exam. Students are strongly encouraged to take the midterm even if unprepared, because it exposes them to the style of questions and helps them identify what to improve. The lecture also emphasizes that the exams are not designed to be clones of prior years. Instead, old exams are open for practice, but deep engagement with course material is presented as more important than pattern-matching on past papers.

### Key points

- The course has two assessment moments: a midterm and a final exam.
- The midterm can count for 20% of the final grade.
- Poor midterm performance can be compensated by better performance on the final exam.
- Students are encouraged to attend the midterm even if they feel unprepared.
- Past exams are available, but relying only on them is discouraged.
- The exam is designed to test understanding of course material, not memorization of past formats.
