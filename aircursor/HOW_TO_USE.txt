AirCursor - external monitor trial

Open the "AirCursor - External Monitor" desktop shortcut.
It starts PAUSED. Press Ctrl+Alt+M to start/pause from any app.
Space also starts/pauses while the camera preview has focus.
Hold a closed fist continuously for 3 seconds to start/resume. The preview counts
down; opening your hand or losing tracking early restarts the countdown.
Open your hand after activation to begin controlling the pointer or CAD view.
A shorter 0.8-second fist hold pauses control. Open your hand before making
another fist; keeping it closed will never toggle repeatedly.

Move: relax/curl some fingers and move the thumb/index midpoint in the green box.
An entirely straight hand is reserved for scrolling up.
Click: briefly pinch thumb and index together, then release.
Double-click: pinch and release twice.
Drag: hold the pinch for 650 ms, then move; release to drop.
Voice: focus a text field in Breadboard, then hold a V sign (index and middle
extended, ring and little fingers curled). Ctrl+Shift stays held while you hold
the V and releases when you stop. Voice starts after a short confirmation;
about 150 ms of tracking flicker is tolerated so it doesn't cut out mid-sentence.
The pointer and clicks stay frozen during the V. Pausing, closing the app,
losing the hand or disconnecting the monitor releases the voice keys.
Scroll DOWN: hold all four fingers bent at the knuckles in the sideways/bent L
shape, keeping the finger joints mostly straight (not curled into a fist).
Scroll UP: hold all four fingers straight, as in your straight-hand screenshot.
Scrolling continues while held and stops when you relax/curl the fingers or
lower the hand. A 0.2-second confirmation prevents brief transitions scrolling.
Click the desired external-monitor app before scrolling.

3D ROTATION AND ONE-HAND PINCH ZOOM
Focus your CAD/3D app and put the pointer over its 3D viewport on the Acer.
Rotation and pinch zoom activate automatically; no three-finger gesture is needed.
The preview shows AUTO 3D and the detected application. Each activation starts
from your current hand position, so switching apps does not jump the view.
Turn/tilt your hand to orbit the VIEW. Open your thumb/index pinch to zoom in,
close it to zoom out. Keep the other fingers relaxed, rather than making a
complete fist or V. In this mode, the pinch does not click or drag objects and
the straight/bent hand poses do not scroll. Switch away from the CAD app or move
the physical pointer outside its window to return to normal pointer/click/scroll.
V still controls voice. A fist pauses the app and exits 3D mode.
Returning to a recognized CAD app resumes automatically. Changing apps/tabs,
losing the hand, or camera timeout releases orbit inputs.

Breadboard's internal parametric CAD artifact is supported too. Focus Breadboard
and move the pointer over the loaded 3D canvas on the Acer. Only the canvas enables
3D controls; moving over its toolbar, parameters or chat returns normal controls.
This requires the updated Breadboard viewer. Typing in a field, a covering dialog,
switching tabs or closing the artifact clears the canvas signal.

Automatic profiles: Blender, SOLIDWORKS, SketchUp, Autodesk Fusion, Inventor,
FreeCAD (CAD navigation), Onshape, Rhino (perspective), Maya, 3ds Max, AutoCAD
(3D), Navisworks and Breadboard CAD. Onshape/SketchUp browser tabs are identified by title.
Use the application's default mouse navigation preset. Rotation is view orbit,
not editing an object's rotation. Arbitrary apps, custom keymaps, 2D drawing
views and non-viewport panels cannot all be recognized automatically.
For custom navigation presets, choose a matching navigation_profile in
settings.json; see NAVIGATION_PROFILES.txt. If zoom direction is reversed by
your app preferences, set invert_zoom to true and restart AirCursor.

Stop immediately: Ctrl+Alt+Esc from any app.
Close normally: Q or the preview window's X.

The pointer maps only to the Acer VG270 G (DISPLAY2). Clicks and scrolling are
blocked outside that screen. Scrolling also requires the focused app to be on
the Acer. Your physical mouse remains free to use all monitors.
If the selected monitor disconnects or its layout changes, AirCursor stops;
it never switches automatically to the laptop or virtual display.

Pinch detection uses palm length, image aspect and available 3D landmarks,
with separate close/release thresholds and two-frame confirmation.
Hand detection filters face regions and duplicate hand detections, and requires
a short confirmation before a new hand can control anything. It keeps the
original detection sensitivity so sideways/bent gestures remain recognizable.
Keep your hand beside your face; if it covers the face, control may stop and
the preview will say "Face ignored". No camera images are saved or uploaded.
Keep Mausely closed while using AirCursor so the two apps do not compete for
the camera, keyboard or mouse.
