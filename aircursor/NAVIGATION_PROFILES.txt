AirCursor 3D view navigation profiles

These profiles emulate documented default mouse controls. They are not plugins
inside the CAD apps, and cannot identify every custom keymap or 3D viewport.
Focus the app and place the pointer over its actual 3D view on the external monitor.
Navigation activates automatically, with no mode-switch gesture. Breadboard's
viewer publishes a canvas-only hint so chat, parameter fields and toolbars keep
normal mouse controls. Other profiles recognize the application, not every panel.

setting       application/preset          orbit input
breadboard    Breadboard internal CAD      Left drag (only over the ready canvas)
blender       Blender                     Middle drag
solidworks    SOLIDWORKS                  Middle drag (parts/assemblies)
sketchup      SketchUp                    Middle drag
fusion        Autodesk Fusion             Shift + middle drag
inventor      Autodesk Inventor           F4 + left drag
freecad       FreeCAD CAD navigation      Hold middle, then left, then drag
onshape       Onshape                     Right drag
rhino         Rhino perspective view      Right drag
maya          Maya                        Alt + left drag
3dsmax        3ds Max default mode        Alt + middle drag
autocad       AutoCAD 3D view              Shift + middle drag
navisworks    Navisworks 3D                Shift + middle drag

settings.json options:
navigation_profile: "auto" normally, or one of the setting names above to
override detection for an app with different bindings.
orbit_gain: 240 by default; smaller values reduce rotation sensitivity.
zoom_gain: 8 by default; smaller values reduce pinch zoom sensitivity.
invert_zoom: false by default; true reverses zoom to match an app's preferences.
scroll_rate: 480 by default (four mouse-wheel notches/second).
Restart AirCursor after changing the file.
