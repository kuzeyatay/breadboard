# Maintained example: Colgate Kitchen

This fictional example is the canonical scenario used throughout the Premortem
tutorial. It is not an actual Colgate initiative.

## Initiative

Colgate launches **Colgate Kitchen**, a grocery line of refrigerated
heat-and-serve entrées led by family-size lasagna.

## Failure statement

It is twelve months after the nationwide launch. Major grocery chains have
removed Colgate Kitchen lasagna and the rest of the entrée line, retail
sell-through missed plan by 70%, $45 million of inventory and launch spending
has been written off, and favorability toward the core Colgate brand has fallen
12 points.

## Stakeholder lenses

- Colgate brand director accountable for growth and core-brand equity
- Grocery frozen-food category buyer accountable for shelf productivity
- Food safety and quality lead accountable for manufacturing controls
- Co-manufacturer operations director accountable for yield and capacity
- Time-pressed parent choosing among familiar heat-and-serve meals

## Example causal path

1. Consumers associate the Colgate name with oral care rather than appetizing
   food (`n001`).
2. That association suppresses first purchase despite broad awareness (`n002`).
3. Weak trial produces sell-through below retailer hurdle rates (`n003`).
4. Retailers reduce facings and then delist the line (`n004`).

## Example mitigation

Before authorizing national production, the consumer-insights director runs a
600-person randomized concept test comparing identical lasagna under the
Colgate name and a standalone food brand. The launch does not proceed under the
Colgate name if branded purchase intent is more than eight percentage points
below the blind concept.

## Example research questions

- How much does the Colgate name change purchase intent relative to an
  otherwise identical unbranded or standalone-brand entrée?
- What weekly unit velocity do grocery buyers require to retain shelf space
  after the introductory period?
- Can the proposed co-manufacturer meet food-safety, yield, and cold-chain
  targets at national volume?
